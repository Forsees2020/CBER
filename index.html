OCOM Student & Academic Collaboration

Prospective Student & Collaborator Pathways

As outlined in our published Cureus (2026) framework, projects in The Campbell Research Lab rely on cloud-accessible, zero-cost bioinformatics and geospatial platforms. OCOM medical students and visiting collaborators can contribute asynchronously or via structured research electives without heavy coding prerequisites.

TRACK 01

Preclinical Medical Student Investigators

Apply the published 3-Phase Drug Discovery Pipeline to characterize novel Plasmodium falciparum targets (sequence alignment, AlphaFold/SWISS-MODEL modeling, Ramachandran validation, and SwissDock screening) toward peer-reviewed co-authorship.

Format: Asynchronous research elective or longitudinal track

Prerequisites: None—stepwise training provided via Cureus protocol

TRACK 02

Geospatial Health & Neuroproteomics Fellows

Join active manuscript teams on Florida Hospital Serviceability (network drive-time modeling & demographic equity overlays) or MTLE+HS Neuroproteomics (PTM consensus mapping & TLDc/thioredoxin structural modeling).

Focus: Follow-up analyses, figure generation & manuscript drafting

Outcome: Co-authorship on regional/national posters & journal articles

TRACK 03

Faculty & Experimental Wet-Lab Partners

We collaborate with clinical educators, public health planners, and biochemical laboratories seeking in vitro validation of top ChEMBL-NTD kinase inhibitors or OXR1/GLRX ubiquitination/phosphorylation mutants.

Scope: Wet-lab validation, curriculum adoption, multi-site grants
